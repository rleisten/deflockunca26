This map was made by Michael Lee, Rebekah Leisten and Jace Arrigali. Our team's aim in creating this database was to illuminate the system of Flock and other ALPR's being used across the state of North Carolina, and specifically on UNC campuses. UNC system universities, especially our own, UNC Asheville, should be more transparent about these cameras and surveillance systems, and we as students should have a say in who can harvest our data.
