What we know: UNC Greensboro

Last checked: 11/13/2026

How long it is kept
Data is purged after 90 days unless a preservation request is filed. Source: DEFlock ILM
Who can search it
According to UNCG's Rekor contract, access to their data is restricted to department personnel. Access for investigative cases is on a case-by-case basis. Source: DEFlock ILM
Who it is shared with
Access can be shared upon written request, and requests are vetted by the ALPR operator supervisor. DEFlock ILM
