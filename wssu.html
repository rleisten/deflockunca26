What we know: Winston Salem State University

Last checked: 11/13/2026

How long it is kept
Under the Flock Safety contract, the retention period is set to 30 Days. The Master Services Agreement specifies that Flock deletes all footage on a rolling thirty (30) day basis, except as otherwise specified on an Order Form. The university is responsible for extracting, downloading, and archiving any footage onto its own storage devices before that rolling 30-day window expires. . Source: https://archive.org/details/deflockilm-uncw-alpr-2026
Who can search it
Access is restricted to authorized department personnel who have completed initial training covering system operation, department policies, and applicable privacy laws. Officers may only search LPR data for legitimate public safety reasons, such as locating vehicles tied to an investigation, identifying individuals involved in criminal offenses or missing-person cases, assessing safety threats, or monitoring secured areas. Searches for personal or non-official reasons are strictly prohibited. UNCW Police may accept search requests from or grant direct database access to outside law enforcement agencies, provided the outside agency has a legitimate "need to know" in the performance of official duties and complies with the department's policy and state law. Source: https://archive.org/details/deflockilm-uncw-alpr-2026
Who it is shared with
The Flock contract provides for both local and national network sharing (e.g., one-to-one, statewide, and national sharing networks across participating law enforcement agencies). UNCW is prohibited from granting "Authorized End User" credentials to individuals who are not officers, employees, or direct agents of the university.
